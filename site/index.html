Yilin Fang

I am a Ph.D. student in the Department of Computer Science and Engineering at The Ohio State University, where I am a member of the W3CIL Lab under the supervision of Dr. Carter Yagemann.

Prior to joining OSU, I earned my Master's degree in Cyberspace Security from the School of Cyber Science and Engineering at Huazhong University of Science and Technology (HUST) in 2023, under the guidance of Dr. Bin Yuan. During my master's program, it was my great fortune to work with Dr. Yueming Wu. I also completed my Bachelor's degree in Information Security at HUST in 2020.

My current research focuses on the intersection of computer security and machine learning, particularly in automated vulnerability discovery, analysis, and prevention.
